IGTrendy Gemini fallback fix

- Article generation now starts with lighter stable Gemini models instead of depending on Gemini 3.8 Flash capacity.
- If a model returns a transient 408/429/5xx error, the server retries briefly and then switches to another model.
- Added Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite and Gemini 2.5 Flash as additional fallbacks.
- Image generation remains optional/non-fatal: an image failure will not prevent an article from being saved or published.
- Trend queue status is correctly marked as drafted after both Draft and Publish actions; the article's own status remains draft/published.

Production env:
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_key
GEMINI_MODEL=gemini-3.5-flash-lite
GEMINI_IMAGE_MODEL=gemini-3.1-flash-image
SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY=your_service_role_key
