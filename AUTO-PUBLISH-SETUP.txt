IGTrendy automatic publishing

- Vercel Hobby cron: daily at 03:30 UTC (09:00 IST, with Vercel Hobby's hourly scheduling window).
- Each run performs one fresh regional Google News scan and then attempts up to 10 independent articles.
- Regions: India, United States, Europe + entertainment/global internet signals.
- Freshness: primarily last 48 hours; older queued topics are not selected by the daily publisher.
- Duplicate protection: source URL + topic similarity against published trends/articles.
- One article failing does not stop the other attempts.
- Images are optional. Openverse is still used when available; publication never fails because images are missing.
- Public News section: /category/news.
- Run supabase/auto_news_10_daily.sql once.
- Vercel environment variables required: CRON_SECRET, and at least one working AI provider key (GEMINI_API_KEY, OPENROUTER_API_KEY, or XAI_API_KEY).
